# CTCUI — Cosmic Theory of Consciousness and Universe Inheritance
## Scientific synthesis, version 6.0 (2026)

**A falsifiable cosmological hypothesis** by Maciej Łuczewski, bringing several open problems
of modern physics into a single frame: the Hubble tension, the nature of dark energy and dark
matter, the black hole information paradox, the fine-tuning of fundamental constants — and the
hard problem of consciousness.

Three interrelated theses: (1) the universe is a node in a generative population of universes,
new universes arising from black hole singularities or from a controlled phase transition of
the quantum vacuum; (2) consciousness is quantum information encoded in the zero-point field,
whose correlations survive the death of the biological carrier and are inherited; (3) the
evolution of the multiverse is an informational process, gradually optimizing conditions for
complexity and consciousness.

34 chapters · mathematical operationalization (the consciousness-information functional ℐ(t))
· three endgame scenarios including **Mitotic Saturation** · seven groups of falsifiable
observational predictions · an honest reply to the consolation objection.
